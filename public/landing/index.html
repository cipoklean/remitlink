FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does my family need a crypto wallet?

No. A passkey on their phone is enough.

What if the link isn't claimed?

The escrow returns the funds to the sender after the link expires.

RemitLink is a hackathon build running on the Monad testnet — demo funds, no real money. Try it at remitlink.vercel.app.
